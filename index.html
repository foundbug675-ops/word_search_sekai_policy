Word Search Sekai

Privacy Policy

Application: Word Search Sekai (com.sheorify.wordsearchsekai)
Effective Date: November 17, 2026 · Version: 1.0.0

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Search Sekai handles information when you play the mobile application on Android. We respect your privacy and designed the game so that you can play immediately without creating an account or providing personal contact details.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All gameplay data is stored locally on your device using an on-device database (Android Room / SQLite) and is not uploaded to our servers. This includes:

  • Completed levels and progress across themes and difficulty modes
  • In-progress word-search boards so you can resume puzzles later
  • In-game virtual coin balance and unlocked theme packs
  • Sound effects and haptic feedback preferences

Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes this local data from your device.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Unity Ads)

Word Search Sekai uses Unity Ads (provided by Unity Technologies) to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements. Unity Ads may automatically collect and process certain technical and advertising data to deliver ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud:

Data Category Examples Purpose
Device & Other IDs Google Advertising ID (AAID), app set ID Advertising, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Approximate Location Country / region derived from IP address Serving regionally relevant ads
App Activity Ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded video completions Analytics and granting in-game coin/hint rewards
App Info & Diagnostics Device model, OS version, language, SDK crash/performance logs Compatibility, reliability, and ad SDK diagnostics

For full details on how Unity Technologies processes data, please review the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions Used

Word Search Sekai requests only minimal install-time permissions needed for ad delivery:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to load and display advertisements when online.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Used by the Unity Ads SDK to access the Google Advertising ID where permitted by your device settings.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise GPS location, or photo/file storage.

5. Your Privacy Controls & Choices

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Play Offline: Core word-search puzzles remain playable offline; rewarded video ads require an active network connection.
  • Delete Local Data: Clear app data or uninstall Word Search Sekai in Android Settings to remove all saved progress and coins.

6. Children's Privacy

Word Search Sekai is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through any third-party service, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
